Q3 · How are the descriptors computed?

SD · Score points

Population SD within an emotion cell
ddof = 0; at least 2 scores
Equal weight per available emotion cell

Hn · Membership ambiguity

Score → Low / Medium / High memberships
−Σ μ log₂ μ / Hmax → cell mean
Equal weight per available emotion cell

Hp · Profile evenness

pe = xe / Σ xe; −Σ pe log₂ pe / 2
All-zero vector → 0
Equal weight per accepted response

Ds / Da · Score points

Ds = reader mean − character mean
Da = |Ds| for each contrast
Equal weight per available contrast

Hmax = 1.018502773018 · Locked membership family · Grid step 0.001

Source: R1 §2.1. Hn and Hp are different quantities; both use the fixed definitions.

The order and weights matter. SD uses the population formula within each available emotion cell, followed by equal cell weights.

For fuzzy entropy, we transform each score through the fixed membership functions before averaging. We do not take the entropy of a mean score.

Profile entropy instead uses equal response weights. An all-zero vector is assigned zero, as is a single-positive-emotion profile.

Target shifts require both targets. We average signed and absolute contrasts separately. Empty cells remain missing.

What stays fixed?

What is resampled?

have SD estimates above their interval upper limits.

Source: R1 §2.4, §3.2 and Table 7. 10,000 resamples; 95% percentile intervals.

These intervals answer a narrow question. How much can a summary change when we resample the accepted responses within the observed cells?

Each cell fixes the configuration, the text, and the evaluation target. We resample the four emotion scores together.

Reader-side and character-side responses are resampled separately. Repetition numbers do not form matched pairs.

The texts, configurations, accepted counts, and missing cells stay fixed. The intervals do not cover uncertainty from missing responses or new texts.

With small samples, this procedure shifts SD downward. In the extension, all six configuration-level SD estimates exceed their interval upper limits.

We keep these results as reported. We do not use the intervals to claim population coverage, statistical significance, or a model ranking.
